## Hi there, I'm Claudia!👋

### About Me
I'm a Software Engineer and Product Designer. My Tech Journey began in La Paz, Bolivia, at Universidad Mayor de San Andrés, where I studied Computer Science and started my career as a Full-Stack developer.  After moving to the United States, I explored different paths and began questioning whether technology was still where I belonged. Discovering UX/UI Design changed that. It allowed me to combine my technical foundation with something I had always enjoyed: understanding people.

Today, I research and design the experience, then build it in code. I design products that are not only functional and feasible to build, but also meaningful, intuitive, and most importantly human-centered.

### 🔭 What I'm up to
- 🏋️ Building **[Lift&Inspire](#)**, a full-stack platform for bodybuilders and powerlifters, whether working with a coach or training on their own; it brings routines, workouts, progress
  tracking, nutrition, and coach-athlete communication into one place, designing and building end-to-end. 
- 💪 Building **ThriveBuddy**, a gamified fitness and wellness app focused on privacy, community, and anti-performative design.
